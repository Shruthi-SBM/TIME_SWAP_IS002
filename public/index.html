What is TimeSwap?

TimeSwap is a professional slot-sharing system designed to solve the problem of unused appointments in services like doctors, salons, and gyms. Many valuable time slots go to waste when users cannot make it, while others struggle to find urgent bookings.

The Problem

Cancellations and schedule changes often leave gaps in service providers' calendars. Without a redistribution system, these slots remain empty, causing revenue loss for businesses and frustration for users who need appointments.
